# Getting started

This guide takes you from a new account to your first completed workflow. It's also the reference example of a **task page** in this framework — copy its structure from [the task template in Writing standards](/framework/standards#page-templates).

## Before you begin

- You need an invitation email from your workspace admin.
- Supported browsers: latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. In the invitation email, select **Accept invitation**.

2. Choose a password.

3. Select **Create account**. You land on the dashboard — your workspace overview.

   <Figure
     src="/img/placeholder/dashboard.svg"
     alt="Dashboard showing the sidebar navigation, three stat cards, and an activity chart"
     caption="The dashboard after first sign-in."
     frame="browser"
   />

4. Open a project from the dashboard to see its tasks. If you have the **Admin** role, you can also create one — see [Managing projects](/guides/managing-projects).

</Steps>

## Verify

You should see the dashboard with your workspace name in the header, and receive a confirmation email within a minute.

## Troubleshooting

<Expandable title="I didn't receive the invitation email">

Check your spam folder first. If it's not there, ask your admin to re-send the invitation — links expire after 7 days.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="The Create account button is disabled">

Your password must be at least 12 characters and include one number. The button enables when all requirements show a green check.

</Expandable>
